# Test Chalkieboard

Help test Chalkieboard before its public release. The test version is distributed through Apple TestFlight.

한국어: [초키보드 테스터 안내](/ko/beta/)

## What you need

- An iPad running iPadOS 18 or later
- Apple Pencil for board writing
- TestFlight from the App Store
- Optional: iCloud Drive on a Mac or Windows PC to prepare lesson materials

## Join the test

Email [chalkieboard@207studio.dev](mailto:chalkieboard@207studio.dev?subject=Chalkieboard%20TestFlight) with the subject “Chalkieboard TestFlight”. Send from the email address where you want to receive the invitation. Applications are handled privately by email; applicant names and email addresses are not published here.

After receiving an invitation, accept it in TestFlight and install Chalkieboard. Send feedback through TestFlight or [contact support](/support/). Include the app version and what happened; avoid sending student information.

## Things to try

Organize PDF, PowerPoint and web materials in a binder, write with Apple Pencil, use classroom tools, and show the student view on a classroom display. Voice control is a beta feature; teacher voice verification is optional.
